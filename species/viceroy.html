Why you might be seeing this now

The point of this card is the look-alike. Florida Viceroys often run dark, Queen-colored. Check for the black line across the hindwing. Willows along fresh water, not milkweed.

Where here

Wet edges, freshwater canals, marshes, preserves. Not a dry lawn butterfly.

Host plants

Willows

Salix spp. especially Carolina willow Salix caroliniana

Look-alikes

Queen, Monarch

The hindwing black line is the mark.
