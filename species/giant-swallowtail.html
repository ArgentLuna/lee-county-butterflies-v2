Giant Swallowtail

Giant Swallowtail

Papilio cresphontes

Common now

Why you might be seeing this now

Cape Coral yards with citrus or wild lime still have orange-dog caterpillars and the big yellow-banded adults. Year-round in Florida. The large one sailing through a backyard in August is usually this.

Where here

Yards, citrus, hammock edges, pineland.
