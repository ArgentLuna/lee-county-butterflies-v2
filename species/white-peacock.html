White Peacock

White Peacock

Anartia jatrophae

Common now

Why you might be seeing this now

This is the butterfly of Cape Coral canals in late August. Water hyssop and frogfruit are still thick on ditch edges, and White Peacocks stay low over that wet ground. People photographed one in Fort Myers today.

Where here

Canal banks, wet ditches, pond edges, weedy yards.

Host plants

Water hyssop

Bacopa monnieri

turkey tangle frogfruit

Phyla nodiflora

Look-alikes

none regular

Whitish wings with brown markings, orange margins, one black forewing spot and two hindwing spots plus a short tail.
