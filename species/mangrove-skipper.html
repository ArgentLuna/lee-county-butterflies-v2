Mangrove Skipper

Mangrove Skipper

Phocides pigmalion

Common now

Why you might be seeing this now

Big dark skipper of red mangrove. Still being seen in mid-August. If you are along a mangrove shoreline, this is the one.

Where here

Mangrove, coastal.

Host plants

Red mangrove

Rhizophora mangle

Look-alikes

Monk Skipper

Monk Skipper is browner, less blue, no white forewing spots.
