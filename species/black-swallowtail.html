Black Swallowtail

Papilio polyxenes

Possible

Why you might be seeing this now

Fennel, parsley, and dill in the garden. Still in season through November. Quieter on iNaturalist this August than the Giants, but a regular yard resident.

Where here

Yards, weedy lots, canal edges.

Host plants

Sweet fennel

Foeniculum vulgare

Parsley

Petroselinum crispum

Dill / carrot / celery

Anethum graveolens / Daucus carota / Apium graveolens

Mock bishopweed

Ptilimnium capillaceum

Look-alikes

Spicebush

Spicebush (not a first-ship regular). Female can look dark; check the orange-centered hindwing spot.
