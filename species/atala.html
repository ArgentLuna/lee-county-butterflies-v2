Atala

Eumaeus atala

Garden colonizer

Why you might be seeing this now

If you have coontie, look here. Atala is not an old Cape Coral hammock butterfly. It is a southeast Florida species that has been colonizing planted coontie on this coast. People are photographing it in Lee County this month, including gardens.

Where here

Gardens with coontie or other cycads.

Host plants

Coontie

Zamia integrifolia / Z. pumila

garden cycads

Look-alikes

none

Black with iridescent blue spots, red abdomen, large red hindwing spot below; flies like a small moth. Unmistakable.
