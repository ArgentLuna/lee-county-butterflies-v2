Cloudless Sulphur

Phoebis sennae

Possible

Why you might be seeing this now

The strongest migrant we will actually watch, but Cape Coral sits south of where most fall Cloudless Sulphurs stop. Late August is early. What you see now is mostly local breeding on partridge pea and senna. The big yellow one high over the street in October is the pulse.

Where here

Yards, weedy lots, roadsides, open parks.

Migration

Yes. Year-round resident plus late-summer/fall buildup. Peak September–October. Most fall migrants stop north of Lake Okeechobee.
