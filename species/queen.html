Queen

Danaus gilippus

Common now

Why you might be seeing this now

Same milkweed yards as Monarch, plus white twinvine. A darker orange-brown “Monarch” in North Fort Myers this week is often a Queen. They live here year-round.

Where here

Yards, pineland, fields, marshes.

Host plants

Milkweeds

Asclepias

white twinvine

Funastrum clausum

Florida milkvine

Matelea floridana

Look-alikes

Monarch, Soldier, Viceroy

Use the hindwing line (Viceroy) and the color (mahogany vs bright orange).
