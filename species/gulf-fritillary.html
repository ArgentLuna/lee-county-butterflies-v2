Gulf Fritillary

Gulf Fritillary

Agraulis vanillae

Common now

Why you might be seeing this now

Passionvine is still going, and late summer is when this species builds up before the fall push south. If you have corkystem or maypop, you are looking at its kitchen.

Where here

Yards, weedy lots, parks, roadsides.

Host plants

Corkystem passionflower

Passiflora suberosa

purple passionflower / maypop

Passiflora incarnata

yellow passionflower

Passiflora lutea

Look-alikes

Julia

Julia is plainer orange and has no silver spots underneath. Gulf Fritillary is silver-spotted below.

Migration

Year-round here, plus a real late-summer and fall pulse into the peninsula. Peak months August–October.
