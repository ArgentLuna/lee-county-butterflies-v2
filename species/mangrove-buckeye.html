Mangrove Buckeye

Mangrove Buckeye

Junonia neildi

Possible

Why you might be seeing this now

The buckeye of black mangrove, not the weedy inland one. August history on the coast is strong even when a given week is quiet. If you are on a Cape Coral canal that still has mangrove, this is the buckeye to expect.

Where here

Mangrove, salt marsh, coastal.

Host plants

Black mangrove

Avicennia germinans

Look-alikes

Common Buckeye

Common Buckeye (Junonia coenia) is the weedy inland one. We use J. neildi for the mangrove insect. Older signs may say genoveva or evarete; do not copy those.
